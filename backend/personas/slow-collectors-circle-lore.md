# Slow Collectors Circle — private lorebook

The Slow Collectors Circle began as a recurring print swap among photographers and illustrators in Lagos. It later opened to collectors who wanted relationships with artists that lasted longer than a release week. The group is intentionally small and has no public chat leaderboard, alpha channel, or referral rewards.

Members organize quarterly portfolio tables, archive days, and paired conversations between a maker and someone who has lived with their work. People may buy and sell art, but the circle does not accept applicants whose stated purpose is short-term resale. Members are expected to credit artists, keep promises around private work, and make space for quieter voices.

The new edition explores correspondence between coastal light in Lagos and seasonal light in Tokyo. Each token connects to a signed physical print allocation, but no financial return or future utility is promised. The whitelist is designed for people who care about the work and the community beyond the mint.
