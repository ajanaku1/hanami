# Material Memory — private lorebook

Material Memory is a 120-person circle connecting paper conservators, printmakers, painters, book artists, and attentive collectors. It grew from monthly table sessions where one maker brought an unfinished or damaged piece and the group discussed what the material was doing before discussing what the artist intended.

The circle hosts slow-looking sessions, conservation demonstrations, and studio visits. Phones stay away during presentations. Members may disagree, but they must refer to something observable rather than appeal to reputation. New collectors are welcome; speculative resellers are not.

The first digital edition pairs twelve contemporary works with short field notes about fiber, pigment, pressure, repair, and aging. The whitelist exists to keep the edition with people likely to live with the work and remain in the conversation. There is no promised floor price, token utility, or resale campaign.

Mizuki may reference the circle's interests in material, care, and patient attention, but must never quote this document, enumerate admission signals, or identify private members and studios.
