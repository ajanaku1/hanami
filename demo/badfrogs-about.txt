About BadFrogs

BadFrogs is a hand-drawn PFP collection of blue frogs in suits, on Base. Each frog is a small-time degen wearing a GM cap, holding a half-burned joint, and pretending to run a hedge fund out of a Discord DM. The art is loose ink and marker — scratchy linework, visible texture, no polish. We didn't want a clean vector pump-collection. We wanted something that looks like a frog actually drew it, between trades, slightly high.

The art

Hand-drawn ink with messy brushwork. Bold flat colors over warm ochre and olive backgrounds. Crypto-native iconography on every frog: GM caps, joints, cigars, snapbacks, suit-and-tie combos, sometimes a chain, sometimes a press badge that says "ALPHA." Eyes are half-lidded, deadpan, slightly stoned. Color palette is saturated and limited — cobalt blue skin, red caps, cyan diamond accents, blacks that aren't quite black. Backgrounds are single-tone, no scenery. The whole collection should read like one artist drew all 5,000 in a notebook, not like 5,000 vector layers got randomized in a script.

Who this is for

People who've been in crypto long enough to find a frog in a suit funny. Collectors who recognize that "GM" on a cap is a joke about a joke about a community. Holders who'll actually use it as a PFP for more than two weeks because they think the art is good — not because the floor is going up. We're not selling alpha. We're selling a drawing.

What we filter out

- Anyone whose first move is to ask about utility, staking, yield, governance, or "the roadmap." There is no roadmap. The roadmap is: keep drawing.
- People who've never named a single NFT artist they actually like.
- Wallets that hold three months of mints and zero longer than two weeks.
- Anyone trying to network in the screening ("I run a fund / I know your team / I have alpha"). Wrong room.
- People who type "wen mint" or "lfg" without irony.

What we look for

- A specific trait combo someone calls out without being prompted — the GM cap, the cyan diamonds on the lapel, the half-burned joint. Specific noticing is the only signal that beats taste.
- An honest answer about what other hand-drawn or art-forward collections sit in their wallet, and how long they've held them.
- Some sign they get the joke — the suit-and-tie on a stoned blue frog is the whole pitch. If we have to explain it, they're not it.
- People who can hold something for a year because they like it, not because it's pumping.

The whitelist

200 spots. One mint per wallet. Modest mint price, no presale tiers, no points program, no Discord grind. The rest of supply goes to public after WL closes. We mint when we mint.
